# Architecture Decision Records

One file per decision, numbered sequentially: `0001-short-slug.md`.

Keep each record short and in the present tense:

```markdown
# 1. Short title of the decision

Date: YYYY-MM-DD

## Status

Accepted | Superseded by ADR-000X

## Context

What forces are at play? What made this a decision rather than a default?

## Decision

What we are doing.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, what we have given up.
```

ADRs are append-only. Superseding a decision means writing a new ADR and marking
the old one superseded — not editing history.
